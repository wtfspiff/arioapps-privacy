Ario Apps privacy request

Delete your data.

This page applies to Ario Apps products, including Hindu: Gita, Bhajan & Puja.

1. Remove information stored on your device

For Hindu, use Android Settings → Apps → Hindu → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. This removes saved passages, reading and worship progress, reminders, downloads, and preferences stored locally.

2. Request deletion from Ario Apps

Email us from your device and include the app name, platform, and any referral or install identifier shown in the app. Do not send scripture activity, documents, photos, passwords, payment card details, or other sensitive content.

Email support@arioapps.com

We will verify the request using the minimum information available and delete matching server-side referral, attribution, or app analytics records controlled by Ario Apps. We normally complete verified requests within 30 days.

What may be retained

We may retain limited records required for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal compliance for only as long as necessary. Aggregated statistics that can no longer be linked to an app installation are not personal data and may remain.

Google advertising data

Advertising and consent data handled by Google is controlled through Android advertising privacy settings, Google account privacy controls, and the consent choices available in the app. Ario Apps cannot delete information held solely by Google.
