Data request

Request deletion

Cricket Live Line: Exchange does not require an account. You can still ask us to locate and delete data associated with your installation or support history.

How to submit a request

  1. Email support@cricketliveline.app with the subject “Cricket Exchange data deletion request”.
  2. Include your app installation identifier if available, the approximate date you used the App, and any support email address you used. Do not send passwords, advertising IDs, integrity tokens or government identification.
  3. We will acknowledge the request and may ask for limited information needed to match records and prevent fraudulent deletion requests.

What is deleted

After verification, we delete or de-identify matching installation preferences, notification records, app analytics we control, search analytics and support correspondence unless retention is required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance or an active dispute. Data held by integrated providers is handled under their documented controls and retention schedules.

Timing and exceptions

We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days. Limited backup copies may remain until normal backup expiry. Aggregated or irreversibly de-identified information cannot be linked back to an installation and may be retained.
