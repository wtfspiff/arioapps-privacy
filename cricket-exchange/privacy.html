Privacy Policy

This policy applies to Cricket Live Line: Exchange, also presented as WYN Cricket, package cricket.exchange.live (the “App”). The App does not require an account to view cricket scores.

Data the App processes

  • Approximate location: country, region or city inferred from an IP address. The App does not request precise GPS location.
  • App activity: launches, screens, tabs, matches followed, notification interactions, ad interactions, settings and feature usage.
  • In-app search: search terms submitted for the search feature and product improvement.
  • App information and performance: crash logs, diagnostics, error messages, response times and app state.
  • Device or other identifiers: installation identifiers, Firebase installation and messaging tokens, advertising identifiers where available and permitted, and integrity-related identifiers.
  • Support information: the email address, message and attachments you choose to send when contacting support.

Why data is used

Data is used to deliver and improve scores, commentary, search, notifications and eligible media; remember app choices; measure reliability and advertising; prevent abuse; comply with law and media-rights obligations; and answer support requests.

Service providers and sharing

We do not sell personal information. Google Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Cloud Messaging may process app activity, identifiers, crash/diagnostic information and messaging tokens. Google Mobile Ads and the User Messaging Platform may process or share IP-derived approximate location, product interactions, diagnostics and identifiers for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention, subject to available consent choices. Google Play Integrity processes app, licensing, request and device-integrity information for security and protected-media controls. Hosting and security providers process the limited information needed to operate the service.

Security and retention

The App blocks cleartext traffic and sends application requests over encrypted HTTPS or secure WebSocket connections. Records are retained only while needed for the purposes above, security, legal obligations and backups, then deleted or de-identified. Integrated providers apply their documented retention schedules.

Your choices

  • Manage match notifications in the App and Android system settings.
  • Use the privacy-choices control, where shown, to revisit available advertising consent choices.
  • Reset or delete the Android advertising ID in Android settings where supported.
  • Request access to or deletion of data we control through the data-deletion page.

Children

The App is a general-audience cricket product and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child supplied personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where required.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will revise the date above. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to support@cricketliveline.app.
