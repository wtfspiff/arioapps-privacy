Privacy Policy

This policy applies to Wowdoku, a puppy-themed logic puzzle game by Ario Apps. The Android package and iOS bundle is com.trine.wowdoku (the “App”). The App has no account or sign-in. Puzzle progress, settings and purchases state are stored on your device.

Data the App processes

  • App activity: app opens, sessions, levels started, completed or failed (level number, board size, time, hints used), screens, feature use, ad views and rewarded-ad completions, and in-app purchase events if purchases are offered.
  • Device and app information: device model, operating system and version, app version, language and time zone.
  • Identifiers: a random installation identifier created by the App. It is not linked to your name or email. Advertising identifiers (Android advertising ID or Apple IDFA) are used by Google Mobile Ads where available and permitted. On iOS this happens only with App Tracking Transparency permission.
  • Approximate location: country or region inferred from your IP address by our analytics server. The App does not collect precise GPS location.
  • Diagnostics: errors and performance information.
  • Support: the email and message you choose to send us.

Why data is used

We use data to run and improve the game, including difficulty, level design and stability. We also use it to measure and show ads, prevent fraud and abuse, and answer support requests.

Service providers and sharing

We do not sell personal information. Ario Apps’ own analytics servers receive app activity, device information and the installation identifier over HTTPS.

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google’s User Messaging Platform may process IP-derived approximate location, ad interactions, diagnostics and identifiers. Google uses them for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention, subject to your consent choices. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Apple and Google process payments if you buy anything in the App.

Security and retention

All requests use HTTPS. Analytics records are kept while needed for the purposes above, then deleted or de-identified. Google applies its own retention.

Your choices

  • Use the privacy-choices or consent form where shown (EEA, UK and other regions where required).
  • On iOS, manage tracking permission in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • On Android, reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Uninstalling the App removes local progress and resets the installation identifier.
  • Request deletion of data we hold through the data-deletion page.

Children

Wowdoku is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child gave us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will revise the date above. Questions and privacy requests can be sent to Ario Apps at support@arioapps.com.
